Privacy

Everything stays on your computer. PIN-Browser does not upload your screenshots, page text, recordings, downloads, settings or usage to anyone, including us. There's no account, no analytics and no tracking.

What PIN-Browser stores

WhatWhere
Screenshots of the window (every 5 seconds while the day log is on), with the page address, title, visible text and scroll position for each one; adult-content check results and blacked-out copiesYour day log folder, one folder per day
Page text for Dial back search (text.jsonl) and copies of pages as they looked (html)The same day folder
Video recordings and downloads (adult ones in a private subfolder)<day log>/<date>/videos and /downloads
Usage Report summaries and video thumbnailsThe same day folder
Lists of recordings and downloadsrecordings.jsonl and downloads.jsonl in the day log folder
Settings, plus logins, cookies and cache, like any browserWindows: AppData\Roaming\PIN-Browser. Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/PIN-Browser
Debug log (debug.log), which includes page addressesThe folder you chose at start-up

Nothing is saved before you choose the day log folder. You can turn the day log off at any time with the Day log button, delete a day in Dial back, or delete the files yourself.

What goes over the network

Only two things:

  1. The pages you visit, exactly like any browser. That includes the site's own pictures and videos, its icon, and video files when you press Download.
  2. Once a week, a download of a public list of adult sites (from The Block List Project on GitHub). This is a download only: a plain request for the public list, with no cookies and nothing about you or your browsing sent along. If it fails, the copy that ships with the app is used.

Nothing is ever uploaded. The adult-content check, the Usage Report, video thumbnails and search all run on your computer.

Websites you visit

Websites still see your visit the way they would in any browser (your IP address, cookies for that site, and so on). PIN-Browser doesn't add anything to that.
