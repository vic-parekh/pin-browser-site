FAQ

Installing

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

That's Microsoft Defender SmartScreen. It shows for apps that aren't code-signed yet, which is the case for this beta. Click More info, then Run anyway. Only do this for the installer you downloaded from the official Releases page.

My Mac says PIN-Browser "can't be opened" or is from an unidentified developer.

That's Gatekeeper. Right-click (or Control-click) PIN-Browser in Applications, choose Open, then click Open. On newer macOS versions, try opening it once, then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this the first time.

Does it update itself?

No. When a new version comes out, download it from the Releases page and install it over the old one. Your settings and day log stay.

Your data

Where is my data?

  • Day log (screenshots, page text, saved pages, recordings, downloads, usage summaries): the folder you chose on first run, one folder per day. You can see or change it in Options > Recording and Day log, and open it with Open folder.
  • Settings, logins and cookies: on Windows in C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Roaming\PIN-Browser (settings are in settings.json there). On a Mac in ~/Library/Application Support/PIN-Browser.
  • Debug log (debug.log): the folder you chose at start-up, usually the day log folder. See it in Options > Recording and Day log.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Everything PIN-Browser records stays on your computer. See Privacy for the details.

How much disk space does it use?

It depends on how much you browse. Dial back shows the disk use for each day, and Delete day removes a day you don't need. Recordings take the most space.

How do I delete something?

Use Delete day in Dial back, or Options > Privacy and NSFW > Clean Up > Remove NSFW. You can also delete files in the day log folder yourself.

Blur and NSFW

How does Blur work?

PIN-Browser checks every day log screenshot for adult content on your computer, using a built-in image model, what the site itself labels NSFW, and a public list of adult sites. With Dial back content set to Blur (Options > General), any page where part of it is NSFW is replaced by a placeholder, in Dial back and in Usage Report thumbnails, and its title and address show as "(hidden: NSFW)". SFW (the default) shows a copy with the flagged pictures blacked out and skips NSFW screenshots that have none. All shows everything.

A normal site is being marked NSFW. What can I do?

Add it to Options > Privacy and NSFW > Never check these sites, then click Clean Up > Re-score to check your saved screenshots again. Screenshots from that site are then no longer NSFW.

Uninstalling

How do I uninstall PIN-Browser?

  • Windows: Settings > Apps > Installed apps, find PIN-Browser, and choose Uninstall.
  • Mac: quit PIN-Browser and drag it from Applications to the Trash.

Uninstalling doesn't delete your day log. If you want it gone, delete your day log folder, and the settings folder listed above, yourself.

Bugs and ideas

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Open New issue on GitHub and pick Bug report or Feature request. For a bug, please include:

  • The version (Help > About shows it) and whether you're on Windows or Mac.
  • What happened, and what you expected.
  • Steps to make it happen again.
  • If you can, the relevant part of debug.log (its folder is in Options > Recording and Day log). Please look it over first: it lists page addresses you visited.
