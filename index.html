A browser that remembers your day

PIN stands for Personal Information Node: a browser that works for you, not just a window onto the web.

PIN-Browser tries out a few ideas that regular browsers don't. It helps you keep a commonplace book, also called a clippings book or scrapbook, of the pages worth saving. It remembers your work, so you can dial back to anything you saw. It keeps you focused with no more than nine tabs, and it shows you where your time actually goes. The goal is simple: help you get more done and spend your time better, with everything kept on your own computer.

What makes it different

Day log

Every few seconds it saves a picture of the window with the page address, title and text, so nothing you saw is lost.

Dial back

Pick a day, scrub through time, search for something you saw, and reopen the page.

Video recording

Records the main video on a page as you watch it, and can save the original video when the site allows it.

Nine tabs, on purpose

Up to nine tabs, each with a timer showing time on screen. Only the tab you're looking at makes sound.

Usage Report

See where your time went each day: active and idle time, top sites and pages, and videos watched.

Stays on your computer

Screenshots, page text, recordings and downloads are saved in a folder you choose. Nothing is sent anywhere else. Privacy
